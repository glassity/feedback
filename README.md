# Glassity Genio feedback

This repository collects feedback from people who use Glassity Genio. Each issue here is a bug report, a request, or an idea sent by a Genio user. The Genio team reviews every one and turns the useful ones into fixes and features.

## How to send feedback

- **From the app:** open your avatar menu in the top-right corner and choose **Send feedback**. Enter a short title and a description. Genio opens the issue here under your GitHub account.
- **From GitHub:** open a [new issue](https://github.com/glassity/feedback/issues/new) in this repository.

Issues from the app get the **Feedback** issue type and the `feedback` label. You'll be notified on GitHub when someone replies or the issue changes.

> **This repository is public.** Anyone can read what you post here. Leave out credentials, customer data and anything else private.

## What makes feedback useful

- What you were trying to do
- What happened, and what you expected instead
- Steps to reproduce it, if it's a problem
- Why it matters to you, if it's a request

## What is Glassity Genio

Glassity Genio is a governed AI workforce for enterprise operations. It turns the AI harnesses Glassity built for its own work into a way for teams to run operations work with AI agents, under human control.

What Genio does:

- **Works with your tracker.** Boards stay in sync with your GitHub issues and projects. Tickets become work that AI agents and people do together.
- **Your choice of agents.** Teams work across agent tools such as Claude Code and Codex, on their own machines or infrastructure.
- **Beyond software delivery.** It covers Cloud Ops, DevOps and platform engineering work, and business operations are being tested next.
- **Governed by design.** It sets standards for how AI is used, keeps people in the loop for decisions, and records results and verification for every piece of work.
- **Traceable.** Every token and decision is traced and can be exported in the FinOps FOCUS format, so AI spend is as visible as cloud spend.

Genio is part of [Glassity](https://glassity.cloud), which also makes the Glassity FinOps platform.
